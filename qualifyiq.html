Status: ● not_eligible
Work Type: contractor
Visa Type: Digital Nomad
Unfortunately, you do not currently meet the mandatory requirements for Costa Rica's Digital Nomad visa. If your situation changes in the future, you may reapply once all requirements are satisfied.
Clarifications
Tax Returns
When formal tax returns are unavailable, immigration authorities may accept alternative evidence that clearly demonstrates income amount, source, and continuity over time.
Alternative Evidence Options:
CPA or accountant income summary letter — Must be prepared by a licensed accountant. Should state: total income earned over the past 12 months, source(s) of income, time period covered.
Year-end income statements — Annual summaries issued by an employer, platform, or accounting system. Must clearly show gross income totals, name of issuing entity, year covered.
Platform annual payout summaries — Official annual earnings reports from recognized platforms. Must include applicant's name or account identifier, total payouts for the year, platform branding or verification markers.
Income History Duration
Costa Rica's Digital Nomad visa requires proof of stable business income for at least 12 consecutive months. This is a mandatory requirement with no exceptions.
Criminal Background — Police Clearance
Costa Rica requires an official police clearance certificate from the country of nationality and recent residence. Police clearance requirements: issued by the appropriate authority, covering the required time period, properly legalized or apostilled if required.
How this is handled:
No record: Certificate serves as proof of a clean record.
Record disclosed: Certificate provides the factual basis used by immigration authorities for discretionary review.
Important boundary:
Costa Rica evaluates on a case-by-case basis. Legal guidance required if a criminal record exists.
