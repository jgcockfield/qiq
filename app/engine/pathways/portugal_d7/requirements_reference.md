## Dependent Rules

- Family members may use the residence visa for accompanying family members applying for a residence visa.
- Additional adults require 50% of the main applicant's minimum means.
- Children under 18 and dependent non-minor children require 30% of the main applicant's minimum means.
- Family route requires relationship documents and financial proof covering each family member.

---

## Discretionary / Refusal Notes

- Missing mandatory documents may lead to refusal.
- Consular services may request supplementary documents.
- False statements may cause refusal or annulment of an already granted visa and legal action under Portuguese law.
- Passive/own-income evidence must be truthful, consistent, and support residence rather than employment or active professional work.

---

## Later-Stage AIMA / Residence Notes

- D7 residence visa is valid for two entries and four months.
- The visa holder must request a residence title from AIMA.
- Residence permit stage generally requires valid passport, residence visa, means of subsistence, and address declaration.
- Permanent residence may require proof of compliance with tax and Social Security obligations.
- Residence authorizations are renewed through AIMA renewal systems/processes.
- Expired documents are not automatically valid after applicable extension periods unless the renewal process is initiated.
