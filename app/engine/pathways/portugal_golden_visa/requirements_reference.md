# Portugal Golden Visa / ARI Requirements Reference

Pathway ID: `portugal_golden_visa`  
Pathway name: Portugal Golden Visa / ARI  
Official pathway name: ARI / Residence Permit for Investment Activity  
Purpose: Determine whether an applicant likely qualifies for Portugal's investment-residence pathway based on current ARI eligibility requirements.

Source basis: Pasted Portugal Golden Visa / ARI official-requirements table as the full source reference.

Important pathway note: This pathway is Portugal's ARI / Residence Permit for Investment Activity. It is an investment-residence pathway, not a D7 passive-income visa and not a digital nomad / remote-work visa.

## Third-Country-National Rule

- ARI is available to qualifying third-country nationals.
- ARI is not available to Portuguese nationals, EU nationals, EEA nationals, Andorra nationals, or Swiss nationals.
- Nationality and passport evidence are required to confirm route eligibility.

---

## Qualifying Investment Routes

The pasted source identifies these qualifying ARI investment routes:

1. Job creation: create at least **10 jobs**.
2. Scientific research: transfer at least **€500,000** into qualifying research activities by public/private scientific research institutions in Portugal.
3. Arts / cultural heritage: transfer at least **€250,000** into artistic production or recovery/maintenance of national cultural heritage through qualifying entities.
4. Non-real-estate investment funds: transfer at least **€500,000** into non-real-estate collective investment units under Portuguese law, with 5-year maturity and at least 60% invested in Portuguese companies.
5. Company capitalization / jobs: transfer at least **€500,000** to create or reinforce a Portuguese company, combined with creating 5 permanent jobs or maintaining 10 jobs, minimum 5 permanent, for at least 3 years.

---

## Real-Estate Exclusion

- AIMA's current ARI page does **not** list real-estate purchase or real-estate rehabilitation as qualifying categories.
- A case relying only on real estate should be treated as disqualifying for this pathway.
- QIQ should not use real estate as the eligibility basis for Portugal Golden Visa / ARI screening.

---

## Core Document and Compliance Requirements

- Valid passport.
- Criminal record certificate from country of origin or permanent residence, issued within the last 3 months, translated into Portuguese and apostilled/consular certified.
- Valid certificate of absence of debts with Portuguese Tax Authority, or proof of non-registration.
- Valid certificate of absence of debts with Social Security, or proof of non-registration.
- Sworn statement confirming compliance with minimum quantitative and time requirements for the investment.
- Personal tax identification numbers from country of origin, residence, or tax residence, or proof none exists.

---

## Disqualifying Factors

- Applicant is Portuguese, EU, EEA, Andorra, or Swiss.
- Investment basis is real estate only.
- Serious criminal conviction: conviction for a crime punishable in Portugal by imprisonment over 1 year.
- Entry/stay ban after removal.
- SII/UCFE refusal alert for refusal of entry, stay, or return.
- Failure to maintain the minimum qualifying investment requirement.

---

## Family Reunification Notes

- ARI holders may benefit from family reunification.
- Family/dependent cases require family relationship documents.
- Portal ARI may be used for family applications connected to the ARI holder.

---

## Portal ARI Process Notes

- Portal ARI is used to register applications.
- Portal ARI is used to upload documents.
- Portal ARI is used to pay fees.
- Portal ARI is used to schedule the AIMA appointment.

---

## Minimum-Stay Requirement

- ARI holders must stay in Portugal at least **7 days in the first year**.
- ARI holders must stay in Portugal at least **14 days in subsequent years**.
- Evidence may include entry/exit records or other travel proof.

---

## Initial Validity and Renewal Notes

- Temporary ARI is valid for **2 years** from issuance.
- ARI may be renewed for **2-year periods** if the applicant maintains qualifying investment requirements.
- Renewal evidence may include updated investment proof, compliance certificates, and criminal checks.

---

## Permanent Residence Later-Stage Notes

- ARI holders and family may request permanent residence if they meet Article 80 requirements.
- Permanent residence is a later-stage pathway and should not be treated as part of initial ARI qualification.
