# Italy Elective Residence Visa Requirements Reference

Pathway ID: `italy_elective_residence`  
Pathway name: Italy Elective Residence Visa  
Purpose: Determine whether an applicant likely qualifies for Italy's Elective Residence Visa based on eligibility requirements.

Source basis: Pasted Italy Elective Residence Visa official-requirements table using Italian government and consular sources as the full source reference.

Important pathway note: This pathway is a passive-income / no-work residence visa. It is not a digital nomad visa and not a remote-work visa.

## Disqualifying Factors

- Employment-based funding: subordinate work income or active work activity cannot support this visa.
- Work in Italy: the Elective Residence Visa does not allow any type of work in Italy.
- No qualifying Italian lodging: some consulates state the application can be denied without a lease, rental contract, or deed in the applicant's name.
- Tourism purpose: the visa is not intended for extended tourism or avoiding Schengen tourist-stay limits.

---

## Consulate-Specific Notes

- Income threshold: approximately EUR 31,000/year is a reference point, but consulates may treat it as a starting parameter rather than a guarantee of sufficiency.
- Housing: Chicago and San Francisco do not accept hotels or multiple bookings as qualifying lodging evidence.
- Passport validity: baseline guidance includes at least 3 months beyond visa/stay; Paris adds issued within the previous 10 years and 2 blank pages; San Francisco requires 15 months from application date.
- Criminal background: San Francisco requires an FBI Identity History Summary issued within the last 6 months.
- Health insurance: Chicago requires 100% medical expense coverage; Paris requires coverage for the full stay, minimum EUR 30,000, hospitalization, emergency repatriation, and remains repatriation.
- Tax returns: some consulates request complete federal returns with schedules; Form 1040 alone may be insufficient.
- Travel: some consulates require a flight reservation or itinerary.

---

## Discretionary Notes

- Submitting all requested documents does not guarantee visa issuance.
- Consulates may request additional documents.
- Financial sufficiency is discretionary and may require more than the stated minimum reference amount.
- Family/dependent cases require sufficient assets and separate documentation for each applicant.
