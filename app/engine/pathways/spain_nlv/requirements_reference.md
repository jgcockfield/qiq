# Spain Non-Lucrative Visa — Requirements Reference

Pathway ID: `spain_nlv`  
Pathway name: Spain Non-Lucrative Visa  
Purpose: Determine whether an applicant likely qualifies for Spain’s Non-Lucrative Visa based on eligibility requirements.

Source basis: Spain Non-Lucrative Visa official-requirements table using official Spanish government sources only.

## QIQ Eligibility Question Areas

QIQ should evaluate eligibility/qualification, not application readiness.

Required qualification areas:

1. No-work intent
2. Non-EU / non-EEA / non-Swiss status
3. Not irregularly present in Spain
4. Passport validity of at least 1 year
5. No disqualifying criminal record
6. No serious public-health disease
7. Spanish-authorized health insurance
8. Passive income/assets sufficient for main applicant
9. Dependent count, if family included
10. Extra funds for each dependent
11. Spanish company ownership/no labor activity, if applicable
12. Public-order/security concern
13. Minor-child schooling issue, if dependents include children

---

## Hard Disqualifying Factors

- Applicant intends to work or perform professional activity in Spain.
- Applicant is EU, EEA, Swiss, or covered by EU free-movement rules.
- Applicant is irregularly present in Spain.
- Passport does not meet the 1-year validity requirement.
- Applicant has a disqualifying criminal record.
- Applicant has insufficient passive income/assets.
- Applicant lacks qualifying health insurance.
- Applicant presents public-order, security, or public-health concerns.

---

## Conditional / Review Factors

- Dependents included.
- Minor children included.
- Funds depend on Spanish company ownership.
- Applicant owns shares in a Spanish company but may perform labor activity.
- Renewal risk if applicant may not reside in Spain more than 183 days per year.
- Medical, criminal, or security issue is unclear.
