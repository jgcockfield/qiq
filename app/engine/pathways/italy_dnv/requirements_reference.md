## Conditional Route Notes

- Digital nomad route: self-employed remote worker; may trigger Partita IVA after entry.
- Remote worker route: employee or collaborator working remotely; requires employment/collaboration contract or binding offer.
- Remote worker employer clean-record route: employer must declare no relevant convictions in the previous 5 years.
- Income source route: New York consulate interprets income as work-derived income from the work to be performed in Italy and does not accept passive income there.
- Passport validity route: Pristina lists 3 months beyond stay; New York lists 15 months beyond intended travel and two blank pages.
- Family route: family reunification may be possible; New York notes spouse and minor children and excludes adult children/parents.
- Renewal route: renewal depends on maintaining work/employment, lodging, health insurance, and employer clean-record condition where applicable.
- Social security route: if no bilateral social-security agreement applies, Italian social-security rules apply.
