## Official Requirements Table

| Category | Requirement | Exact eligibility rule | Evidence required | Status | Official source URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Visa type | Study stay over 90 days | Allows a non-EU foreign national to remain in Spain for more than 90 days for approved studies, student mobility, volunteering, or training. | Student visa / study-stay application. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Applicant type | Non-EU / non-EEA / non-Swiss | Applicant must not be an EU, EEA, or Swiss citizen, or a covered family member. | Passport / nationality proof. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Immigration status | Not irregular in Spain | Applicant must not be irregularly present in Spain. | Current lawful status or consular filing from residence country. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Application route | Usually consular | Application is generally filed in the applicant's country of origin/residence through the Spanish consulate; some higher-study cases may apply from Spain if legally present. | Consular application or proof of lawful status in Spain. | Mandatory / conditional | Ministry guide — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Timing | File before study start | Application must be submitted at least 2 months before studies begin, unless justified exception applies. | Admission date / program start date. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Passport | Valid passport | Passport or travel document must be valid, recognized by Spain, and have at least 1 year validity. | Passport copy + original at filing. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Study admission | Accepted program | Applicant must be admitted to an authorized/recognized institution or center. | Admission letter. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Study type | Full-time recognized studies | Higher studies must be full-time and lead to a recognized higher-education qualification; post-compulsory secondary studies must be full-time and lead to a recognized title. | Program details, institution recognition, enrollment. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Modality | In-person/hybrid requirement | Higher studies may be in-person or hybrid; non-university studies require at least 50% in-person. | Program modality confirmation. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Tuition/enrollment | Enrollment paid or proven | Applicant must prove admission and payment of registration/enrollment fees; if timing prevents payment, a responsible declaration may satisfy the requirement temporarily. | Enrollment receipt, payment proof, or declaration. | Mandatory / conditional | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Financial means | 100% IPREM monthly | Applicant must have funds for stay and return: 100% IPREM per month, unless accommodation is prepaid for the full stay. Study-cost funds are not counted. | Bank statements, scholarships, family support, grants. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Dependents income | Extra funds for family | 75% IPREM monthly for first family member and 50% IPREM monthly for each additional family member. | Financial proof for family unit. | Conditional | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Health insurance | Spain-authorized insurance | Applicant must have health insurance with an insurer authorized in Spain, with benefits similar to Spain's basic public health system. | Health insurance certificate/policy. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Medical certificate | Public-health clearance | Applicant must prove they do not have diseases with serious public-health implications under the 2005 International Health Regulations; not required for some in-Spain applications. | Medical certificate. | Mandatory / conditional | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Criminal background | Required if stay exceeds 6 months | If stay exceeds 6 months and applicant is of criminal age, applicant must have no criminal record in Spain or countries of residence during prior 5 years. | Criminal record certificate from country of origin/residence. | Conditional | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Public order | Not rejectable/security risk | Applicant must not be listed as rejectable in treaty countries and must not pose public order/security/public-health risk. | Background screening. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Work rights | Limited student work | Higher-study authorization automatically allows work; other categories require additional work authorization. Work cannot exceed 30 hours/week and must remain compatible with studies. | Study category; employment/self-employment authorization if needed. | Conditional | Ministry guide — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Dependents | Family allowed only for higher studies/specialized health training | Eligible family may accompany only if main applicant holds higher-study or specialized health-training stay. | Marriage/partnership, birth, dependency/disability evidence. | Conditional | Ministry guide — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Dependents work | Family cannot work | Family members under student-family stay are not authorized to work. | Family authorization status. | Conditional | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Residence card | TIE if over 6 months | If stay exceeds 6 months, applicant must apply for TIE within 1 month after entry/concession. | TIE application. | Conditional | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Duration | Linked to studies | Higher-study stay equals total official duration of studies; post-compulsory secondary study stay equals study duration, capped at 1 year. | Program duration. | Mandatory | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Renewal / extension | Extension allowed | Extension may be requested 2 months before or 3 months after expiry if studies continue. | Passport, funds, insurance, admission/enrollment/payment, fee receipt. | Conditional | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Extension limit | Up to 1 year per extension | Extension validity matches enrolled course duration, capped at 1 year; up to two extensions for higher and post-compulsory secondary studies. | Continued enrollment. | Conditional | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
| Document formalities | Translation/legalization | Foreign documents must be translated into Spanish/co-official language and public foreign documents legalized or apostilled unless exempt. | Sworn translation + apostille/legalization. | Mandatory where applicable | Ministry of Inclusion / Migraciones — https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios |
