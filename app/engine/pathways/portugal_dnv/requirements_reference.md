# Portugal Digital Nomad Visa Requirements Reference

Pathway ID: `portugal_dnv`  
Pathway name: Portugal Digital Nomad Visa  
Official checklist label: D9 Residency Visa – Remote Work  
Common private-market label: D8 Digital Nomad  
Purpose: Determine whether an applicant likely qualifies for Portugal's remote-work residence route based on eligibility requirements.

Source basis: Pasted Portugal Digital Nomad / Remote Work Visa official-requirements table using Portuguese government, embassy, and AIMA sources as the full source reference.

Important pathway note: Portugal's official Washington checklist labels this as D9 Residency Visa – Remote Work, while many private sites call it D8 Digital Nomad. In QIQ, use the pathway name `Portugal Digital Nomad Visa`. This pathway is for professional activity performed remotely for entities outside Portugal. It is not a passive-income visa and not the Portugal D7.

## Temporary-Stay vs Residence Route Distinction

- Residence route: residency visa allows 2 entries, is valid for 4 months, and requires applying for a residence permit with AIMA after entry.
- Temporary-stay route: temporary stay visa allows remote work / digital nomad stay under 1 year and multiple entries.
- This pathway folder is intended for the Portugal Digital Nomad Visa residence route unless a future pathway explicitly separates the temporary-stay route.

---

## Employee vs Freelancer / Business-Owner Route Distinction

- Remote employee route: subordinate work requires an employment contract, promise of contract, or employer declaration confirming the labor link.
- Freelancer / business-owner route: independent professional activity requires a company/society contract, service contract, written service proposal, or document proving services to one or more entities.
- In both routes, the professional activity must be performed remotely for entities outside Portugal.

---

## Income Rule

- Applicant must prove average monthly income over the last 3 months equal to at least 4 Portuguese monthly minimum wages.
- Portugal's 2026 minimum wage is EUR 920/month.
- The 2026 remote-work income threshold is 4 × EUR 920 = EUR 3,680/month.
- Evidence may include last 3 months income proof, payslips, invoices, contracts, and bank statements.

---

## Tax Residence Certificate Requirement

- Applicant must provide a document attesting fiscal residence.
- This requirement is mandatory in the official documentation list.

---

## Dependent / Family Route Notes

- Family members may apply as accompanying family members of a residence visa applicant.
- Qualifying family may include spouse/partner, minor children, dependent adult children studying in Portugal, dependent ascendants, and other listed dependents.
- Accompanying family visa requires stable and regular means sufficient for the applicant and relatives for the stay or 12 months, whichever is less.
- Relationship proof and funds evidence are required.

---

## AIMA Residence Permit and Renewal Notes

- After entry with a residence visa, the applicant applies to AIMA.
- AIMA residence stage requires valid passport, valid remote-work residence visa, work/service declaration, and residence-address declaration.
- AIMA says the temporary residence authorization for remote work is valid for 2 years.
- The residence authorization is renewable for successive 3-year periods.
- Updated eligibility documents may be required at renewal.

---

## Disqualifying Factors

- Facts that would have prevented visa issuance.
- Conviction for a crime punishable in Portugal by more than 1 year imprisonment.
- Active entry/stay ban after removal.
- SII/UCFE refusal alert.
- False statements may cause refusal or annulment of visa and legal action under Portuguese law.

---

## Missing-Document / Discretionary Review Notes

- Missing mandatory documents may lead to rejection.
- Consulate may request supplementary documents.
- Complete, truthful, consistent documentation is required.
